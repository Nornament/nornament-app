Scenarios

A scenario is a way of turning cost into an asking price. One is the default for the whole catalogue; a single piece can be switched to another if you allow it. Gold always prices from the live rate and making always from the figure on the line — a scenario cannot touch either.

Switching scenario on a piece
Switching on the piece changes what that one piece is quoted at, for everyone, and is written to the audit log with who and when. It is not a private preview — someone who can switch can discount, which is why Sales is unticked by default.
A showroom given only Retail sees one price and cannot discover the wholesale one — the scenario is applied in the database, not chosen in the browser, so switching role in the page cannot reveal it.
