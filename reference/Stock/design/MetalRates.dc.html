Fetching from smbullion.in

I looked. It is not a page with numbers on it.

No rates in the HTML
The page is 2.5 KB and carries no prices. Rates arrive over a socket.io stream, zlib-compressed, as symbols gold, silver, xauusd with bid and ask.
Nothing here can hold a socket
A Supabase function answers one request and stops. A live feed needs something that stays connected, or a job that dials in, takes one tick and hangs up.
It is their feed
That stream is SM Bullion's commercial product, undocumented and unauthenticated by accident rather than invitation. Reading it on a schedule is taking it.
What I would build instead. The rate box stays typed, with the date-stamp it already has and an audit entry for every change — ten seconds each morning, which is what the ticker at the top of the app was always for. If you want it automatic, ask SM Bullion for a feed; most dealers will give a customer one, and then it is a supported integration rather than something that breaks the first time they change a field name.
What changes underneath. A purity now belongs to a metal, and a metal has its own live rate. A material in the Metal category points at one of them. Platinum or palladium later is a third card on this screen and no code change. Changing a factor moves every sale price that derives from it immediately, and moves no frozen cost at all.
